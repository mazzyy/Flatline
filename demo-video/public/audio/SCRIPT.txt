Every online shop monitors its servers. Almost none monitor their money.

When a checkout breaks on a Friday evening, or refunds suddenly spike, most merchants find out days later, from a payout report or an angry customer.

This is Flatline: an on-call SRE agent for your PayPal revenue.

It listens to PayPal webhooks and checkout telemetry. When a metric breaks its baseline, four agents take over. Triage scopes the incident. The Diagnostician reads PayPal data through a read-only MCP server and finds the root cause. The Remediator proposes fixes. And every proposal goes through a policy proxy written in plain code, not a prompt. Only the executor behind it can write.

Let's break something. I ship a bad currency config. Checkouts start failing, and within seconds an incident opens. The Diagnostician points to the exact config version that caused it, with evidence. A warning banner goes up automatically. The rollback needs my approval. I approve, failures stop, and the Verifier confirms recovery.

Now a refund wave. The agent finds one product behind most of the refunds and proposes pausing it. It also tries a bulk refund. The policy says no. Agents can ask; code decides.

For failed subscription renewals, a reminder goes out on its own. Capturing the outstanding balance waits for a human.

It runs only in the PayPal sandbox, agents never hold write credentials, and every step is in the audit log.

Built on PayPal's REST APIs, MCP server and JavaScript SDK, with Azure-hosted models.

Flatline. On call for your revenue.
